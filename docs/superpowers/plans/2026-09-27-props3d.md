# 3D props: design record

> Built natively in one session on 2026-09-27, tests first. This file records the design as built, for the reviewer and for the next session. It is not a task list.

**Goal:** 3D hats, sunglasses and pests on the live camera and in the photo editor. In the editor, three fingers turn a sticker in depth.

**Operator decisions (2026-09-27):** live camera and photo editor. Own dock tab. A flat sticker under three fingers tilts like a card. One finger drags and two fingers scale and turn, as before.

**Stack:** Three.js 0.186 (`GLTFLoader` from `three/examples/jsm`), no new dependency. Art: 11 models by Astra, CC0, brief R5.

## Global constraints

- Zero third-party requests at runtime. The CSP of the production build stays as it is.
- Licences: the models are CC0 (released by the operator). No new package.
- Kid UI: icons, 64 px tap targets, no reading.
- Colours of the camera picture pass through unchanged. A picture with no prop is the same bit for bit as before.
- Photo, video and screen show the same: motion depends on the frame time only.

## Live camera

**Stage space.** x right, y up, z to the viewer. One unit is half the picture width. Orthographic camera. The picture is not mirrored here: the warp pass mirrors it with all that is on it.

**Place and turn.** Place and size come from the landmarks, as for flat stickers. Only the turn comes from the pose matrix. Measured in a spike: a prop placed by the matrix alone misses the face by 9 to 36 px on a 640 px picture, because the matrix fits a generic head.

**Depth, measured on a real face** (in distances from cheek to cheek):

| Landmark | Depth |
|---|---|
| Nose tip (4) | +0.28 |
| Bridge of the nose (168) | +0.10 |
| Forehead (10) | +0.08 |
| Chin (152) | -0.03 |
| Outer eye corners (33, 263) | -0.07 |
| Sides of the face (234, 454) | -0.50 |

The zero is on the skin, not in the middle of the head. The middle of the head in depth is where the sides of the face are. The first version took the zero as the middle. Result: the hidden head shape stood half a face width too far to the front, hid the sunglasses and the ladybug, and cut the hats.

**Width.** `head.width` is the face width of `faceon.ts`: cheek to cheek, or 0.867 of the face height when that is more. It holds when the head turns to the side. All rule numbers are in this unit.

**Hidden head shapes (`OCCLUDERS`).** Two ellipsoids per head write depth and no colour, before every prop. Shape 1 is the face up to the hairline, as deep as a head. Shape 2 is the top of the head, thin (0.2 deep). Reason: the hats are toys, their opening is smaller than a real head. A top as deep as a head cuts the front of the brim and the band. The thin top still hides the back of a brim and a pest behind the head. Both shapes are a little smaller than a head: too large hides what is worn, too small only shows a pest a moment longer at the edge of the head.

**Rules.** Hats hang on landmark 10 with an offset in the frame of the head (up, and 0.55 back: over the middle of the head, so the hat stays on the head when the head turns). Sunglasses hang on landmark 168. Pests: `orbit` (around the middle of the head, leaning 0.45 rad to the viewer so the back and the wings show), `crawl` (on the skin around landmark 151), `hang` (beside the head, swings).

**Edges.** The first render target has 4 samples while a prop is on, and none with no prop. Three.js builds the target again after `dispose()`. Measured with `ffmpeg -lavfi psnr`: the picture with no prop is the same bit for bit, before and after a prop was on.

**Colours.** The first pass holds sRGB values with no colour space on the target. A lit material gives linear values. `likeTheCamera` replaces `colorspace_fragment` with the sRGB transfer function and turns tone mapping off.

## Photo editor

**Model.** `EditorSticker` has `rot` (turn in the plane, 2D canvas), `yaw` and `pitch` (turn in depth), `model` (id of a 3D prop). Order of the turns: yaw around the line that stands in the sticker, then pitch around the line that lies on the screen, then `rot` and the mirror on the 2D canvas.

**Gesture.** Three pointers: the way of their middle turns the selected sticker (`tilt`). A way to the right turns the front to the right. A way down tips the top to the viewer. A way as long as the short side of the photo is half a turn. A 3D prop: yaw wraps, pitch stops at 90 degrees. A flat sticker: both stop at 75 degrees (`MAX_TILT`), so the card is never a line.

**Drawing.** `Shots.draw` renders the sticker alone (3D prop, or the flat picture on a plane) with a perspective camera of 30 degrees on a transparent canvas of its own. `renderEditor` draws that canvas at once with `drawImage`. The shot shows 0.9 units to every side (`FRAME`), one unit is the long side of the sticker, so the sticker fits in every turn and has the same size as the flat one at no tilt. The shot size follows the sticker in steps (128 to 1024 px). A flat sticker with no tilt takes the old 2D path, pixel for pixel. With no WebGL a tilted flat sticker is drawn flat and a 3D prop is left out.

**Mirror.** The 2D canvas mirrors the shot. The yaw of a mirrored sticker is drawn with the other sign, so the gesture keeps its direction.

## Review focus

1. A model fails to load (offline first start, broken cache): no prop, no error loop, a retry after 5 s. Editor: no sticker is added.
2. WebGL context limits on a phone: the editor adds one context. `Shots.dispose` gives it back (`forceContextLoss`).
3. Two faces: every face has its own instances and head shapes. A face that leaves takes its props with it.
4. Samples switch on a slow phone: the target is built again when the first prop goes on and when the last goes off. One frame of cost, no flash expected. Not verified on a phone.
5. A pose matrix of zeros (no pose yet) gives no turn, not NaN.
6. Three fingers, then one finger lifts: the two-finger gesture takes over with no jump (it reads the places before the event). A three-finger touch is never a two-finger tap.
7. Production CSP: models with textures need `fetch` from a `blob:` address, which `connect-src 'self'` stops. The import refuses textures until that is solved.
